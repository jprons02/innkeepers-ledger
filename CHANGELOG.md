# Innkeeper's Ledger

## Unreleased

- Sign the guestbook at the inns you visit, and collect the signatures of travelers you meet.
- **Signing:** talk to an innkeeper and choose "Sign the guestbook". Compose your entry
  from a phrase builder in eight voices, pick a wax seal you've earned, and sign. Each
  inn takes one signature from you per week.
- **The ledger:** type /ledger to open your book. The Inns tab lists every inn you've
  signed, and each inn's page holds your signatures and those of travelers who stayed
  there. "Read the guestbook" at an innkeeper opens that inn's page.
- **Collection:** see how many inns you've signed, by continent and zone, with a stamp
  for each inn.
- **Travelers:** when you group with, or share a guild with, other players who use the
  AddOn, your books swap entries. Only each player's own signatures are shared, and
  never during combat.
- **Cosmetics:** quills and wax seals unlock as your collection grows. All are earned
  through play.
- **Share:** /ledger share gives you a copyable text string of your ledger. Including
  the travelers you've met is your choice, and it starts off.
- Your ledger is saved per character in your game's saved variables. The AddOn has no
  network access: sharing with other players happens only in-game.
